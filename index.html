Ordering Eggless Cakes in Pune

Are all cakes at Pune Artisan Bakes 100% eggless? ▼

Yes, our kitchen is strictly 100% pure vegetarian and egg-free. We do not use eggs, gelatin, or animal-derived emulsifiers in any of our sponges, frostings, or decorations.

How much notice is required for a custom birthday cake order? ▼

For standard single-tier signature cakes, 24 to 48 hours notice is recommended. For sculpted fondant cakes, tiered milestone cakes, or weekend events, we recommend booking 4 to 7 days in advance.

Do you offer home delivery in Pune? ▼

Yes, we provide door-to-door temperature-controlled delivery across all major Pune and PCMC areas. You can also opt for self-pickup from our studio in Kothrud or Koregaon Park.
